Jinash Rouniyar

Student and software builder · Atlanta, GA

I created this site as a home for the work I do and a place to explain why I do it. Most of my time goes into applications: tools that help people search, write, learn, or make sense of data. A personal page felt necessary because those projects live in different repos and hackathon write-ups, and I wanted one page that ties them back to the person who made them.

The motivation is simple. I am a student at Georgia State University, and building is how I learn. Internships, research, and side projects taught me that I care less about a finished demo and more about whether something is actually useful. That is why I keep making sites and applications — each one is a chance to take an idea I wish existed and put it in front of someone else.

I also wanted a page that feels like me, not like a template. The colors and fonts in the stylesheet are a small palette I chose on purpose: warm paper, ink, and copper. If you want to see more of the work, the links below go to my GitHub, LinkedIn, and the rest of my site.
